## Classification boundary

- The direct folder mappings for woodworking, food and sewing follow the approved taxonomy direction.
- Thin circular and straight cutting forms from the packaging folder were placed under paper slitting/cutting because this is the closest of the six approved application categories. The source label remains visible.
- Three visibly heavy rectangular or trapezoidal machine knives were placed under plastic recycling as candidate similar-product cases.
- Shapes whose application cannot be established from appearance alone were placed conservatively under custom industrial. In particular, the perforated discs, forked/profiled pieces, curved serrated piece, triangular/pointed pieces and hand-held forms are not presented as confirmed paper or plastics capability.
- Exact suitability always requires drawing, sample, equipment and operating-condition review through the RFQ path.
