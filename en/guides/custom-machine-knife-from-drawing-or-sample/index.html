01 / CUSTOM MACHINE KNIVES

Custom Machine Knives: Fit, Edge and Cutting Conditions

A blade is not defined by its outline alone. Mounting dimensions, edge geometry, material and operating conditions determine whether it fits the machine and performs as intended.

Review the essentialsCustom knife range→
SUMMARY

Align the fit and cutting conditions first

Start with the mounting datums, hole pattern, thickness and critical dimensions. Then review the workpiece material, cutting motion and the problem seen on the machine. If an existing blade is used as the reference, allow for wear and previous regrinding. Once the sample is approved, retain the final drawing number and inspection requirements for repeat orders.

SPECIFICATION

Four inputs define the specification

Clear information at this stage makes quoting, sampling and repeat supply more straightforward.

01

Machine fit

Datums, hole pattern, thickness, clearance and mounting direction.

02

Source reference

Drawing revision, sample condition and any difference between measured dimensions and the original print.

03

Cutting conditions

Workpiece material, thickness, cutting motion and the issue observed in production.

04

Approval standard

How the sample will be accepted, plus the final drawing, specification and inspection requirements.

COMMON BUYER QUESTIONS

Three details often missed when copying a used knife

Why not manufacture directly from the measured dimensions?

A used knife may be worn, distorted or repeatedly reground. Unworn features, installation space and the original drawing help establish the intended dimensions.

Which dimensions matter most for machine fit?

Focus on datums, hole pattern, thickness, clearance, mounting direction and adjacent components. Complex profiles also require a clear distinction between mounting and cutting faces.

How do repeat orders stay consistent?

After sample approval, retain the drawing number, critical dimensions, material, hardness, edge geometry and inspection requirements. Create a new revision if the operating conditions change.
