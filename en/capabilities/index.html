CONTROLLED PROCESS

Eight manufacturing and control stages

Real production media from the Zhangzhou base shows the route from incoming material checks to protective packaging. The exact route remains subject to blade geometry, material and approved technical requirements.

  1. 01

    Material Verification

    Handheld analysis equipment supports applicable material checks. Final grades and requirements follow controlled technical documents and inspection records.

  2. 02

    Blank Shaping

    Laser cutting and other blank-shaping processes follow the approved drawing, with subsequent operations planned around geometry and material.

  3. 03

    Heat Treatment

    Blade blanks enter heat treatment under an approved process route. Temperature profiles and detailed process parameters are not published online.

  4. 04

    Machining

    Machining is planned around mounting interfaces and geometric requirements. Feasible ranges and tolerances require drawing review.

  5. 05

    Precision Grinding

    Applicable grinding processes form the cutting edge and critical surfaces. The grinding route is confirmed for each blade geometry and application.

  6. 06

    In-process Inspection

    Gauges and inspection equipment are used to check applicable features during production against the approved drawing and process requirements.

  7. 07

    Final Inspection

    Finished blades are checked against the approved drawing and acceptance requirements, while applicable batch records remain controlled.

  8. 08

    Protective Packaging

    Cleaning, edge protection and packaging are selected for the blade and delivery conditions, with labels and batch information managed to project requirements.

Media is selected to show working methods without disclosing controlled drawings, inspection records or process parameters.
