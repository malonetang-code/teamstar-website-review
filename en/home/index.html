FROM ENQUIRY TO DELIVERY

A clear path from your requirement to a blade ready for use

The manufacturing route changes with the blade. The points that keep a custom project on track remain clear.

  1. 01

    Share the need

    Start with a drawing, sample, or application and the issue that needs solving.

  2. 02

    Agree the direction

    Align material, geometry, critical dimensions, and acceptance priorities.

  3. 03

    Make to the requirement

    Use the manufacturing route suited to the blade and project.

  4. 04

    Check what matters

    Inspect relevant dimensions, material, hardness, edge condition, or other agreed features.

  5. 05

    Protect the delivery

    Identify, protect, pack, and hand over the blades with the appropriate information.
