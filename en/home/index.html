ABOUT TEAMSTAR

Industrial knife heritage, connected to manufacturing in Zhangzhou

Teamstar, also known as Qunxin Industrial, is a member of Wei Qun Cutting Tools Group. The group’s cutting-tool business began in Taiwan and brings more than 40 years of industrial knife experience. A Shenzhen production base was established in 1990.

Qunxin Industrial was established in Zhangzhou in 2024 to manufacture and sell industrial machine knives. Today, a project can begin with a drawing, a physical sample, or the actual application.
